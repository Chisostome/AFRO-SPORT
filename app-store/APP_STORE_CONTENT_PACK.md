## App Review Notes — copy/paste template

### Review notes
AFRO SPORT is a sports information application focused on African football and related sports data.

The main public sports content can be viewed without signing in.

Some account-based features require authentication. A dedicated review/demo account should be entered in the App Store Connect App Review Information section rather than in this repository.

For the review team:
1. Open the app and review the public sports content.
2. Review available matches, league standings, teams, players and top scorers.
3. If account access is needed, use the demo account supplied in App Store Connect.
4. Backend services required by the app are intended to remain available during review.

No payment or in-app purchase is required for the core sports experience in this version.

If a feature is not available for a particular competition or date, this reflects the current sports data available to the application.
